I dream of the day I can delete all my social media accounts.

Not because I hate the internet — I love it. I love connecting with people, sharing ideas, and discovering new perspectives. But social media has become something else entirely.

It's designed to keep you scrolling, not thinking. To make you react, not reflect. To show you what's outrageous, not what's meaningful.

I'm not there yet. I still use it to stay connected with friends across the world. But someday, I'd like my connections to be more intentional — through email, through this site, through real conversations.

Until then, the best way to follow along is through my [email newsletter](/) or [RSS feed](/feed.rss).
